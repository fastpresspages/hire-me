Privacy

Short version: no cookies, no analytics, no list.

Effective 24 October 2026

What this page collects

If you send the contact form, you choose to share your email address and a message. That is the only personal data this page asks for. Include your name or company in the message only if you want to.

How it is delivered

The form posts to FormSubmit, a free form-to-email service, which forwards the message to meetcode99@gmail.com. FormSubmit processes the message on its own infrastructure; review its privacy policy if you need details. I keep the email only as long as needed to reply and follow up.

The page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep standard request logs (IP address, user agent, time) under its own privacy statement. I do not receive or query those logs.

What this page does not do

  • No cookies, local storage, fingerprinting or advertising pixels.
  • No analytics or session recording.
  • No newsletter, drip sequence or list rental.
  • No selling or sharing your message with anyone else.

Fonts and external links

The page loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives the request needed to serve them. Links to GitHub, LaunchGrade and YouLauncher open other sites with their own policies.

Removing your data

Email meetcode99@gmail.com with the address you used and I will delete the message and reply thread unless I am required to keep it.
